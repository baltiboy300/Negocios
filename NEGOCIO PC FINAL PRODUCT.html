Hardware reference

Drone atmospheric profilingVertical soundings of humidity and temperature during calm windows flag a boundary layer turning unstable — an early precursor to convective cloudburst development.
LIDARGround-based or airborne LIDAR resolves fine-scale terrain change — slope bulging, crack widening — between repeat surveys.
Ultrasonic AWSUltrasonic anemometers and disdrometers have no moving parts, so they keep reporting wind and rain rate through icing and violent storms.
Infrasound arraysDebris flows and large rockfalls generate low-frequency infrasound that can arrive seconds to tens of seconds before the hazard itself.
