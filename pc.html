Key Regulatory Discrepancies (FSSAI vs EU)

TBHQ (Tertiary Butylhydroquinone)

Permitted by FSSAI in oils and fried snacks up to specified limits. Restricted or heavily scrutinized in European union child formulations due to potential immune system impacts in long-term studies.

Titanium Dioxide (E171)

Banned as a food additive across the European Union since 2022 over genotoxicity concerns. Continues to be utilized in various confectionery and whitening agents under Indian FSSAI guidelines.
